# **Problems Tackled:-**



1. **Chinese is a very vast language with lots of ambiguity. Finding the correct translation model thus was the foremost and the most important task we had. Out of all the available models, we went ahead with the Helsinki-NLP model, which is a dedicated Chinese to English translation model, unlike other model available.**
2. **Adding the translated text directly onto the original PDF was not straightforward, as it would lead to cluttering. We explored a few solutions and finally came to the idea of covering the original text with a white box and then overlaying the translation text onto that. This made sure to keep the output PDF as clean as possible.**
3. **The translations of the Chinese text into English often resulted into larger than original text. Tackled using legend implementation.**
4. **Fitting The legend into the PDF was another task to tackle. To fit it perfectly, we made the legend size dynamic, fitting it according to the largest text to accommodate.**
